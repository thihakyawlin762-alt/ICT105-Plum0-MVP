## Slide 2: Problem and Target Users

### Problem
University students often struggle to manage multiple assignments across different courses, resulting in missed deadlines, poor organization, and increased academic stress.

### Target Users
- University Students
- Lecturers

---

## Slide 3: Evidence from Customer Discovery and Validation

- Students reported difficulty tracking assignments from multiple courses.
- User testing showed that centralized assignment management improved organization.
- Feedback highlighted the usefulness of assignment search, dashboard summaries, and progress tracking.

---

## Slide 4: Solution and Value Proposition

### Solution

Smart Assignment Tracker is a web-based assignment management system that allows students to organize assignments, monitor deadlines, track progress, and enables lecturers to review assignment records and course progress.

### Value Proposition

- Better assignment organization
- Easier deadline tracking
- Improved productivity
- Centralized academic management
