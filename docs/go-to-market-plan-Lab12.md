# Digital Go-to-Market Plan

## Product / Prototype Name

*Smart Assignment Tracker*

---

## Target Early Users

The first target users are university students at Rangsit University who regularly manage coursework, assignments, projects, and deadlines. These students often struggle with organizing multiple assignments from different courses and need a simple digital solution to improve academic productivity.

The secondary target users are lecturers who want to monitor student assignment progress, review submission records, and gain a better overview of class activities through a centralized dashboard.

---

## Main Value Proposition

*Smart Assignment Tracker helps students stay organized, meet assignment deadlines, and improve academic productivity while providing lecturers with a simple platform to monitor assignment progress and classroom performance.*

# Launch Sequence

### Phase 1 – Prototype Preparation

- Complete the Lab 12 landing page.
- Finalize the Smart Assignment Tracker prototype.
- Verify all links, navigation, forms, and dashboard functionality.
- Prepare screenshots and supporting documentation.

---

### Phase 2 – Internal Testing

- Test the complete user journey.
- Verify assignment creation, editing, searching, completion, and dashboard functions.
- Collect feedback from team members.
- Fix usability issues before public demonstration.

---

### Phase 3 – Initial Release

- Share the landing page with classmates.
- Demonstrate the prototype during ICT105 laboratory sessions.
- Allow students to explore the assignment management workflow.
- Introduce the Lecturer Dashboard functionality.

---

### Phase 4 – Feedback Collection

- Record comments from lecturers.
- Gather usability feedback from classmates.
- Identify confusing navigation or missing features.
- Prioritize improvements based on user suggestions.

---

### Phase 5 – Prototype Refinement

- Improve the user interface.
- Enhance navigation consistency.
- Optimize dashboard usability.
- Refine documentation and screenshots.
- Prepare the final prototype submission.

---

# Risk and Mitigation

| Potential Risk | Impact | Mitigation Strategy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Students may not understand the system immediately | Lower engagement during demonstrations | Design a simple landing page with a clear explanation and navigation |
| Users may overlook important features | Reduced prototype effectiveness | Highlight key features using icons, feature cards, and Call-to-Action buttons |
| Bugs during live demonstrations | Negative impression of the prototype | Perform complete testing before presentations and prepare backup screenshots |
| Limited user feedback | Less opportunity for improvement | Encourage classmates and lecturers to provide suggestions after demonstrations |
| LocalStorage limitations | Prototype data only exists on one browser | Clearly explain that LocalStorage is used only for educational prototype purposes |
| Time constraints before final submission | Some planned improvements may not be completed | Prioritize core functionality, documentation, and usability improvements before optional enhancements |

---

# Expected Outcomes

The Digital Go-to-Market Plan aims to introduce Smart Assignment Tracker to its intended users, validate the usefulness of the prototype, collect meaningful feedback, and demonstrate the project's value as an academic productivity solution.

Although developed for the ICT105 course, the prototype demonstrates how a simple web-based assignment management system can improve organization, reduce missed deadlines, and support both students and lecturers in managing academic activities more efficiently.
