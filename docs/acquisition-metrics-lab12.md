# Acquisition Metrics

## Purpose

This document defines how the Smart Assignment Tracker team will measure early user interest, landing page performance, and prototype engagement during Lab 12. These metrics help evaluate whether the landing page successfully attracts university students and lecturers, encourages them to explore the prototype, and provides useful feedback for future improvements.

---

# Required Metrics

| Metric ID | Metric Name | What It Measures | Formula / Counting Method | Data Source | Target / Success Criterion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| M-01 | Landing Page Views | Number of visitors who open the Smart Assignment Tracker landing page | Count total page visits | Landing Page / Manual Tracking / Browser Analytics | At least *30* page views |
| M-02 | CTA Click Rate | Number of users who click the *Explore the Prototype* button | (CTA Clicks ÷ Landing Page Views) × 100 | Landing Page JavaScript / Manual Tracking | At least *40%* click rate |
| M-03 | Prototype Demo Visits | Number of users who successfully open the Smart Assignment Tracker prototype | Count prototype launches | Prototype Link / Local Demo Logs | At least *20* prototype visits |
| M-04 | Feedback Responses | Number of users who provide comments or suggestions after testing | Count completed feedback responses | Feedback Form / Manual Collection | At least *10* responses |
| M-05 | User Conversion Rate | Percentage of visitors who continue from the landing page to the working prototype | (Prototype Visits ÷ Landing Page Views) × 100 | Landing Page + Prototype Tracking | At least *60%* conversion |
| M-06 | Returning Visitors | Number of users who revisit the landing page or prototype after their first visit | Count repeated visits manually or through browser tracking | Manual Tracking / Browser Analytics | At least *5* returning users |

---

# Interpretation

The acquisition metrics help the team understand how effectively the Smart Assignment Tracker attracts and engages its intended users. If the landing page receives many visitors but few users click the call-to-action button, the headline, visual design, or value proposition should be improved. If users visit the prototype but do not continue exploring its features, the navigation, usability, or interface should be simplified. Low feedback participation may indicate that the feedback request is difficult to find or not clearly explained, so the team should make the feedback section more visible. If conversion rates remain low, the landing page content, messaging, or demonstration flow should be revised to better communicate the benefits of the system. These metrics will be reviewed during future development to guide design improvements and prepare the Smart Assignment Tracker for the final prototype presentation.
