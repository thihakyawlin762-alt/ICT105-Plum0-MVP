# Sales Scenario

## 1. Project Title

Smart Assignment Tracker

---

## 2. Target User / Customer

### Primary User:
University students who need to manage multiple assignments, deadlines, and coursework across different subjects.

### Secondary User:
Lecturers who need to monitor student assignment progress and review assignment records.

### Decision-Maker or Admin Role:
Lecturers and course instructors who use the Lecturer Dashboard to review assignment records, monitor completion status, and analyze student progress.

---

## 3. Situation / Context

A university student is taking several courses during the semester. Each course has different assignments, deadlines, and requirements. The student currently tracks assignments using paper notes, messaging applications, or memory, making it difficult to stay organized.

As the semester becomes busier, the student begins forgetting assignment deadlines and struggles to monitor overall academic progress. At the same time, lecturers have limited visibility into assignment completion and student workload without a centralized tracking system.

---

## 4. Pain Point

Students experience several challenges:

- Missing assignment deadlines.
- Difficulty organizing assignments from multiple courses.
- Lack of a centralized system for tracking progress.
- Spending time searching through chat messages or notes.
- Limited visibility of upcoming tasks and priorities.

Lecturers also experience challenges:

- Difficulty monitoring assignment completion.
- Limited access to overall student assignment progress.
- No centralized dashboard for assignment analytics.

---

## 5. Product Introduction

The Smart Assignment Tracker is introduced as a web-based assignment management platform that helps students organize assignments, monitor deadlines, track completion progress, and receive reminders through a centralized dashboard.

The system also provides a Lecturer Dashboard that allows instructors to review assignment records, monitor student progress, and view assignment analytics.

---

## 6. Value Message

For university students who experience difficulty managing assignments and deadlines, our Smart Assignment Tracker helps them stay organized, monitor progress, and avoid missed submissions by providing assignment management, deadline tracking, search functionality, notifications, and dashboard analytics in one centralized platform.

---

## 7. Sales Conversation / Pitch Moment

Team Member:

"Managing assignments across multiple courses can become overwhelming, especially during busy periods of the semester. Many students rely on notes, memory, or scattered chat messages, which increases the risk of missing deadlines.

Smart Assignment Tracker solves this problem by giving students a single platform where they can add assignments, track deadlines, search records, monitor completion status, and receive reminders. Lecturers can also monitor assignment progress through a dedicated dashboard.

Our goal is to help students stay organized, improve productivity, and reduce missed assignments while providing lecturers with better visibility into academic progress."

---

## 8. Expected User Action

After viewing the product demonstration, users are expected to:

- Visit the landing page.
- Try the prototype demo.
- Add a sample assignment.
- Explore the student dashboard.
- Search and edit assignment records.
- Test assignment completion features.
- View lecturer dashboard analytics.
- Provide feedback for future improvements.

---
